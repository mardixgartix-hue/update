Returns & Refund Policy

Home/Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Given the strictly regulated nature of pharmaceutical formulations, returns and refunds are governed by statutory drug safety protocols.

1. Damaged or Discrepant Deliveries

In the unlikely event of transit damage or packaging discrepancy, notifications must be submitted to our claims desk within 48 hours of delivery along with photographic verification and lot numbers.

2. Replacement & Credit Notes

Verified claims for transit-damaged goods will be processed promptly with replacement stock or institutional credit notes issued to your account.

3. Expired or Temperature Compromised Stock

Expired stock returns are processed according to our standard institutional return authorization agreements. Contact your regional distribution coordinator for batch return clearance.
